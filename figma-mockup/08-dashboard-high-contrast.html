Accessibility notes — Dashboard in High contrast + Large text

Same screen as 02 with High contrast and A+ turned on from the toolbar: black background, white text, yellow highlights, every border 2px, text 15% larger. All pairs exceed WCAG AAA (7:1).

  1. 1"Skip to main content" link appears on the first Tab press, so keyboard and screen-reader users don't have to tab through 10 menu items on every page.
  2. 2Status never relies on colour alone. Every EUI status has a unique shape (✓ circle, ▲ triangle, ✕ octagon, dashed circle), a word, and the exact number. Critical/High bars also use stripes, for colour-blind users.
  3. 3Plain-language explanations. Instead of only "EUI 284", users read what it means and what to do. This helps non-technical staff and users with cognitive disabilities.
  4. 4Charts have a text summary and a data-table view. Screen readers read the summary. Every bar has its value written on it, and the current month is marked with a pattern, not only a different colour.
  5. AAccessibility toolbar on every page: text size, high contrast, read-aloud (text-to-speech) and English/Cebuano.
