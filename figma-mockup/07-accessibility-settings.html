Accessibility notes — Accessibility Settings

  1. 1Seeing (low vision, colour blindness): text size, high contrast, dark mode, a blue/orange colour-blind palette, and underlined links.
  2. 2Hearing & listening: text-to-speech for users with low vision or reading difficulty, and visual alerts for Deaf or hard-of-hearing users.
  3. 3Moving & typing (motor disabilities): reduced motion, bigger targets, voice input for meter readings, and a longer session timeout with a warning (WCAG 2.2.1 Timing Adjustable).
  4. 4Reading & understanding (cognitive, language): English/Cebuano, simple explanations, and a dyslexia-friendly font. Switches show ON/OFF in words, not only by colour or position.
  5. 5Keyboard shortcuts are listed on screen and can be opened anytime with "?". The live preview shows how each setting will look before saving.
