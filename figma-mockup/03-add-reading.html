Accessibility notes — Add Meter Reading (OCR)

  1. 1Step indicator with words and numbers. "Step 3 of 3" is written out and announced, so users always know where they are and how much is left (cognitive accessibility).
  2. 2Photo has alt text and a caption. Screen-reader users hear the reading the photo shows. The large drop zone and "Take photo" button are easy to hit for users with tremors.
  3. 3No one is forced to use the camera. Users who can't hold a phone steady can type the reading, or say it with voice input.
  4. 4Uncertain digits are flagged with shape + word + colour, and each digit box has its own label (for example "Digit 6 of 6, uncertain"). The confidence is explained in plain words.
  5. 5Prevents mistakes before saving. The used kWh, the cost, and a "looks reasonable" check are shown before saving, with Back and "needs review" options (WCAG 3.3.4 Error Prevention).
